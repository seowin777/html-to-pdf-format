HTML to PDF Format — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

What the extension does with your data

The extension saves the page you are looking at, a block of it, a "clean" article, HTML code you paste, or an .html file you open, as a PDF on your computer. It has no server and makes no network requests of its own: the page, the code and the files are processed only inside your browser and are never uploaded anywhere. The extension itself sends no analytics.

It reads the open tab only when you click the extension icon (Chrome's "active tab" access): the title and address for the card in the popup, and the page content to print it. To make the PDF it attaches Chrome's debugger to that tab for a few seconds; Chrome shows a "debugging" bar meanwhile. Pasted HTML lives in the memory of the editor tab and is not written to disk, except for the final PDF.

It stores in chrome.storage.local on your computer only: your settings (mode, paper size, orientation, margins and the switches), the "Clean page" state, flags that the first-run hints were shown, and counters for the "rate this extension" prompt. Nothing is synced.

The PDF is saved by an ordinary browser download into your usual download folder. Pictures and styles of a page you paste are loaded by Chrome itself, as when you open any web page.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • We do not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Website analytics

The welcome and goodbye pages on this website use Google Analytics to count installs and removals. The extension itself contains no analytics code.
